  exchanges that `apply_combat_result` later discards on a weapon break.
- **Medium — `can_attack_from_tile` missing the `_equipped_can_attack` guard** (AI
  positions a `basic`-profile healing-staff enemy as an attacker).
- **Low ×4** — enemy `start_of_turn` skills never fire; `HUD._displayed_unit` dangles
  after death; stale `attacker_skills_blocked (unused)` schema comment; preview omits
  Vantage ordering.
