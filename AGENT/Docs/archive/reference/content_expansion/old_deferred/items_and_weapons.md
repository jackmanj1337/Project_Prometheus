# Fire Emblem TTRPG — Weapons & Items

> **Weapon Triangle:** Swords beat Axes, Axes beat Lances, Lances beat Swords. Dark beats Anima, Anima beats Light, Light beats Dark. Advantage: +10 Accuracy, +2 Damage. Disadvantage: −10 Accuracy, −2 Damage.  
> **Effectiveness:** A weapon effective against a unit treats its Mt as **3× higher** when fighting that unit.  
> **S-Rank Bonus:** +10 Hit, +5 Crit, +1 Damage with that weapon type.

## Forging

Weapons can be modified at purchase if a forge is available (GM discretion). Staves cannot be forged.

| Stat | Increment | Max Change |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Mt | ±1 per increment | ±5 |
| Hit | ±5 per increment | ±25 |
| Crit | ±3 per increment | ±15 |
| Wt | ±1 per increment | ±5 |

**Total modifications per weapon: 20** (5 per stat × 4 stats)

| Increment | Cost | Cumulative Total |
|-----------|------|-----------------|
| 1st | 150 gold | 150 gold |
| 2nd | 300 gold | 450 gold |
| 3rd | 450 gold | 900 gold |
| 4th | 600 gold | 1,500 gold |
| 5th | 750 gold | 2,250 gold |

Maxing all 4 stats costs **9,000 gold** (in addition to the weapon's base cost).
