## Optional Rule: Pair Up

*Pair Up is the signature mechanic of Awakening, allowing two units to share a space.*

### How Pair Up Works

Two allied units in the same space form a **Pair**. One unit is the **Lead** (active, acts normally) and one is the **Support** (passive, cannot take independent actions).

- The Support unit is not a separate target — enemies cannot directly attack the Support unit.
- The Lead unit moves for both.
- A unit can join or leave a Pair at the start of their turn before acting. Leaving a Pair places the departing unit in an adjacent empty space.

### Stat Bonuses

While paired, the Lead unit gains a bonus based on the Support unit's class type:

| Support Class Type | Bonus |
|-------------------|-------|
| Physical (STR-primary) | +2 STR |
| Magical (MAG-primary) | +2 MAG |
| Fast (SPD 9+) | +2 SPD |
| Armoured/Mounted | +2 DEF |
| Support (Cleric, Bard, Heron) | +2 LUK and +2 RES |

At GM discretion, a mutual support bond (A-rank or higher) may increase these bonuses by +1.

### Dual Strike

When the Lead unit makes a successful attack: **SKL/2%** chance the Support unit also attacks the target for **50% normal damage**. The Dual Strike does not trigger counterattacks.

### Dual Guard

When the Lead unit is attacked: **SPD/2%** (of the Support unit) chance the Support unit steps in and **nullifies all damage** from that one attack. Dual Guard can trigger at most once per combat.

### Pair Up Skills

The following skills interact with Pair Up (see Awakening Skills Supplement):

| Skill | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Dual Strike+ | +10% Dual Strike activation chance |
| Dual Guard+ | +10% Dual Guard activation chance |
| Dual Support+ | Stat bonuses granted to Lead unit increase by +1 |
