# Tactical RPG — Awakening Skills Supplement

> This supplement adds skills from *legacy tactical-RPG source material* not present in the base handbook.
> Skills already present in the handbook (Vantage, Wrath, Miracle, Nihil, Renewal, Astra, etc.) are not repeated here.
> Skills marked with **\*** are activated by choice.
> All existing rules for skill limits (max 4 per unit) apply unless the GM extends them.

---

## Generic Skills

These skills are available to any unit and can be found, awarded, or purchased at GM discretion.

> **Note on duplication:** Several skills appear in both this section and in the Class-Specific section below. The Generic listing means the skill can be awarded or purchased outside of its class context (at GM discretion). The Class-Specific listing defines the class it is normally tied to and at what point it is granted.

## Class-Specific Skills

These skills are tied to specific Awakening classes. They follow the same rules as class-specific skills in the base handbook — granted at a set level or upon promotion, and not freely transferable between classes unless the GM permits it.

---

### Veteran
*Class: Tactician — from start*

This unit gains **1.5× EXP** from all sources (rounded up). This applies to both combat EXP and staff/ability EXP.

---

### Rightful King
*Class: Great Lord — Promotion Skill*

All of this unit's percentage-based skill activation rates (SKL/2%, LUK%, STR/2%, etc.) increase by **+10%** before calculating.

> Example: A unit with SKL 14 would normally have a 7% Sol activation rate. With Rightful King, it becomes 17%.

---

### Ignis
*Class: Grandmaster — Promotion Skill*

SKL/2% during this unit's attacks: If attacking with a weapon (STR-based), add half of this unit's MAG to the damage calculated against the target's DEF. If attacking with a tome (MAG-based), add half of this unit's STR to the damage calculated against the target's RES.

---

### Rally Spectrum
*Class: Grandmaster — Occult Skill*

As an action: All allies within 3 spaces gain **+2 to all stats** for 1 full round. This unit's turn ends after using this skill.

---

### Anathema
*Class: Dark Mage — Starting Skill*

Enemy units within **3 spaces** suffer **−10 Hit and −10 Dodge** during combat.

---

### Vengeance
*Class: Sorcerer — Promotion Skill*

SKL/2% during this unit's attacks: Add **half of all damage this unit has received so far this map** to this attack's damage. This bonus resets at the start of each map.

---

### Tomebreaker
*Class: Sorcerer — Occult Skill*

+50 Hit and +50 Dodge when fighting a unit equipped with a Tome.

---

### Lifetaker
*Class: Dark Knight — Promotion Skill*

After this unit defeats an enemy on **its own turn**: Restore HP equal to **half the damage dealt** in the killing blow.

---

### Shadowgift
*Class: Dark Knight/Sorcerer — Occult Skill*

This unit may equip and use **Dark tomes** regardless of proficiency rank or class restrictions. wEXP is still gained normally. This skill does not grant proficiency — it only permits use.

---

### Beastbane
*Class: Taguel — Laguz Skill (at promotion level)*

+50 Hit and +50 Dodge when fighting units with the **Beast, Mounted, or Armoured** special qualities.

---

### Stoneborn
*Class: Taguel — Occult Skill*

SKL/2% at the start of each of this unit's turns while in animal form: Choose one enemy within 2 spaces. That enemy's DEF and RES are reduced by **4** until the start of this unit's next turn.

---

### Dragonskin
*Class: Dragonkin — Laguz Skill (at promotion level)*

All damage this unit receives is **halved** (rounded down). Additionally, this unit is **immune to weapon effectiveness** — no weapon deals bonus damage against this unit based on special qualities.

---

### Wyrmsbane
*Class: Dragonkin — Occult Skill*

+50 Hit and +50 Dodge when fighting units with the **Dragon, Mounted, or Armoured** special qualities.

---

### Special Dance
*Class: Dancer — Starting Skill*

Once per turn, as an action: Target 1 adjacent ally who has **already acted this turn**. That ally may immediately take another **full turn**. Additionally, choose one stat (STR, SPD, SKL, or LUK); the target gains **+4** to that stat until the start of the next turn.

- Special Dance cannot target the same unit consecutively.
- Cannot target the Dancer itself.
- This unit's turn ends after using Special Dance.

---

### Charm
*Class: Lord / Bride — Starting Skill / Promotion Skill*

Allied units within **3 spaces** gain **+10 Hit and +10 Dodge** during combat on their turn and on the enemy's turn.

---

### Galeforce
*Class: Dark Flier — Promotion Skill*

After this unit **defeats an enemy** on its own turn: This unit may immediately take **another full turn** (move and act again). Galeforce can only trigger **once per map turn**.

---

### Sol
*Class: War Monk / War Cleric — Promotion Skill*

SKL/2% during this unit's attacks: **Restore HP** equal to half the damage dealt in that attack.

---

### Acrobat
*Class: Trickster — Promotion Skill*

This unit treats all terrain movement costs as **1** (forests, mountains, deserts, etc. all cost only 1 movement). This does not allow movement through impassable terrain.

---

### Pass
*Class: Trickster — Occult Skill*

This unit may move **through enemy-occupied spaces** as though they were empty during its movement phase. Enemies in the path cannot counterattack during the movement. This unit still cannot **end** its movement on an enemy-occupied space.

---

### Aggressor
*Class: Dread Fighter — Promotion Skill*

+10 damage when **this unit initiates combat** (player-phase attacks). Has no effect when this unit is the defending unit.

---

### Swordfaire
*Class: Dread Fighter — Occult Skill*

+5 damage when this unit attacks with a **Sword**. This bonus stacks with weapon might and all other modifiers.

---

### Deliverer
*Class: Griffon Rider — Promotion Skill*

This unit suffers **no stat penalties** (SPD, SKL) and **no movement penalty** when rescuing an allied unit. Effectively grants the Savior skill (handbook) as a built-in class bonus, with the additional benefit that this unit's movement is also unaffected.

---

### Lancebreaker
*Class: Griffon Rider — Occult Skill*

+50 Hit and +50 Dodge when fighting a unit equipped with a **Lance**.

---

### Odd Rhythm
*Class: War Monk/War Cleric — Occult Skill*

This unit gains +15 Hit and +15 Dodge on **odd-numbered turns** of the map (turns 1, 3, 5, 7…). Has no effect on even turns.

---

### Even Rhythm
*(Alternative Occult — assign to War Cleric if differentiating from War Monk)*

This unit gains +15 Hit and +15 Dodge on **even-numbered turns** of the map (turns 2, 4, 6, 8…). Has no effect on odd turns.

---

### Rally Magic
*Class: Dark Flier — Occult Skill*

As an action: All allies within 3 spaces gain **+4 MAG** for 1 full round. This unit's turn ends after using this skill.

## Designer Notes

**On the Faire and Breaker families:** These skill families can make certain combat matchups very swingy. The GM may wish to restrict players to one Faire and one Breaker skill at a time, or rule that Faire and Breaker skills count as two of the four skill slots each, to preserve balance.

**On Galeforce:** This is one of the most powerful skills in Awakening and warrants careful GM attention. Consider restricting it to once per full game session rather than once per map turn, or requiring it to cost two skill slots.

**On Rally skills:** In Awakening, Rally skills end the user's turn. This is already noted above. GMs running large parties may find Rally Spectrum particularly powerful; consider limiting its effect to +1 to all stats rather than +2.

**On Pair Up skills:** Dual Strike+, Dual Guard+, and Dual Support+ are only relevant if the GM implements the Pair Up optional rules from the Awakening Classes Supplement.
