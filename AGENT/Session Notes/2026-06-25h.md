## 1. Why now / the connection
The `[DSP-14]` `force_onto_invalid` ring-out we just firmed can **kill a unit out of combat, with no
clear killer, possibly holding a Key Item** — exactly the case the owner wants covered. Grounding: death
funnels through `unit.handle_death()` from `CombatResolver`, but **nothing disposes of the inventory**,
and **non-combat death causes don't route through any shared path** (grep clean for drop/droppable/
on-death-inventory). Real gap.

## 2. The pinned rule set
An **optional `CampaignRules` death-inventory profile** (F4) — author default + per-case overrides:
- **Modes:** `to_convoy` (no loss) · `lost` (destroyed) · `drop_on_tile` (recoverable pickup) ·
  `transfer_to_killer` (loot).
- **Single disposition path:** route ALL death causes through one `handle_death` inventory step —
  combat, **F5 condition/poison ticks, hazard terrain, `[DSP-14]` force_onto_invalid, scripted/event
  death** must funnel through the same hook (today only combat calls `handle_death`).

## 3. Edge cases enumerated (owner's list + adjacent)
1. **Death out of combat** (no exchange).
2. **Death without a clear killer** — `transfer_to_killer` has no recipient → fall back to the profile's
   no-killer disposition.
3. **Death holding a Key Item** — Key-Item **locks** (`[CEX-14..16]`) **override** disposition: never
   `lost` (recovered to convoy / re-granted) even under a harsh profile. *(The critical safety rule.)*
4. **No convoy on this map** → `to_convoy` fallback (pending stash / drop-on-tile).
5. **Casual / Phoenix** (#12) — a returning unit gets its inventory back (not truly dead).
6. **PvP / skirmish** — no permanent loss.
7. **Simultaneous deaths** (AoE wipe) — per-unit resolution + drop ordering.
8. **Enemy death** — loot / `drop_on_tile`, incl. the "droppable-item enemy" case.
9. **Death while carrying** (`[DSP-5]`) — carried unit drops per DSP; carrier's own inventory follows
   this rule.
