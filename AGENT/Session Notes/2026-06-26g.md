## 1. Two architectural findings
- **Cover hooks a DIFFERENT phase than redirect.** Redirect reads post-mitigation (emits a new effect
  through the new target's pipeline); cover needs the **protector's own DEF/RES** to mitigate the
  **original** attack, so it must intercept **pre-mitigation** and swap the defender. → two distinct
  `CombatResolver` hooks: **cover = pre-mitigation defender-resolution**, **redirect = post-mitigation
  `effect_applied`**. New forward-req: the combat pipeline must expose the earlier hook.
- **Whole-exchange cover = provoke.** Re-targeting the attacker onto the protector is aggro-control =
  `[PRV]`/`[STY-17]` provoke (resolved 2026-06-25p). Per-hit keeps cover purely defensive and
  complementary (a provoked enemy charges the healer; the knight covers the blow).

## 2. Owner calls (AskUserQuestion, 2026-06-26)
- **Substitution = per-hit intercept** (asked for a provoke explanation first; confirmed). To-hit/crit vs
  the **original target**; on a landing hit, **mitigation + HP vs the protector**. NOT whole-exchange.
- **Scope = damage + conditions + displacement.** Displacement = the shove **vector re-applied to the
  protector from its own tile** (ally stays put), via DSP collision/validity; all-or-nothing.

## 3. Provoke (`[PRV]`/`[STY-17]`) — explained for the contrast
Diplomacy/aggro: a directed 3-state `relationship(from,to)` matrix; one `set_relationship(from,to,stance,
scope)` primitive with two callers (the `set_relationship` MET action + the reactive `provoke_on_attacked`
flag), two layers (faction-pair + per-unit), two scopes (map/campaign). Changes who an AI *decides* to
attack (pre-decision); stance gates initiation, counter happens regardless. Use cases: lone-villager
aggro, army aggro, make-peace, faction-joins-mid-battle, "kingdom declares war." Distinct from cover's
mid-combat damage interception.

## 4. Resolutions (CVR-1..6)
- **CVR-1** pre-mitigation hook, upstream of redirect; protector's own redirect can fire post-mitigation;
  reassignments **non-coverable** (one hop). **CVR-2** default full; `share` `[REQ-16]` fraction term
  (each side through own mitigation); non-scalar = all-or-nothing. **CVR-3** `[STY-9]` selector +
  deterministic priority (author key, fallback nearest→unit-id); coverer must be valid;
  **`cover_if_lethal`** flag (default true = sacrifice; inverse of `fires_on_death`); RDR-13 resource gate
  applies; no protector → no-op. **CVR-4** protector's mitigation vs original attacker, original damage
  type/on-hit, **kill-credit+EXP → attacker**, ally no EXP; **forecast shows the protector as defender**;
  AI reads the same forecast. **CVR-5** protector death rides `[RDR-8]` snapshot-then-resolve; ally
  unharmed, no turn/position change. **CVR-6** damage (per-hit) + conditions (protector takes it instead)
  + displacement (vector→protector from its tile, DSP rules).
