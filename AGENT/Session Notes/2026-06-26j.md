## 3. Grounding for that answer (so it starts warm)
**The prediction engine = the combat forecast:**
- `CombatResolver.preview_combat(attacker, defender) -> Dictionary` — **scripts/core/CombatResolver.gd:553**.
- `CombatResolver._collect_combat_modifiers(context, preview: bool, dry_run: bool)` — **:134**; threads
  `preview` into `SkillHandler.apply_trigger(u, "...", context, preview, false, dry_run)`.
- UI: **scripts/ui/AttackPreview.gd** (`show_preview` :112, forecast rows :268).

**KEY FINDING — a real-vs-preview split ALREADY EXISTS (the answer's foundation):**
- `preview_combat` calls `_collect_combat_modifiers(context, true, true)`. Per the in-code comment
  (:557–561): **`preview=true` applies deterministic skills (Resolve, Wrath, Faire …) so the forecast is
  accurate but EXCLUDES random-activation skills** — those are **hidden from the forecast yet still fire
  in real `resolve_combat()`**. `dry_run=true` = SkillHandler does **not** persist limited-use counters
  during a preview (no side effects).
- ⇒ "hide a feature from the prediction without hiding it from reality" is **already an established
  pattern** here (the `preview` flag + the deterministic/random split), **not a new capability** — the
  detour is really about **generalizing/parameterizing** it.

**Connects to the interceptor work:**
- **`[ICP-4]`** — the interceptor forecast must be a **pure dry-run of all three phases incl. counter**;
  reactions (cover/swap/redirect) are exactly the kind of thing you'd hide to **bait** the AI.
- **`[CVR-4]`/`[RCT-1]`** — "AI target-eval reads the **same** forecast." So **forecast fidelity = AI
  capability**: feed the AI a lower-fidelity/blinded prediction and it makes bad trades / walks into traps.
- **`[STY-10]`** generalized effect forecast · **`[REQ-15]`** projection/preview API.

**Design angle to explore in the answer (don't pre-decide):**
- A per-feature **`forecast_visibility`** knob, with **three distinct audiences** to keep separate:
  (1) hide from the **AI's evaluation** (difficulty lever — dumber AI plans on an incomplete prediction);
  (2) hide from the **player's displayed forecast** (information/fog — surprise, bait);
  (3) hide from **both** (a concealed trap/reaction).
- Determinism caveat: hiding from the *forecast* must NOT change the *resolution* (the real path stays
  canonical + replay-safe); only the projection's inputs are filtered. The existing `preview`/`dry_run`
  split is the precedent for keeping resolution authoritative.
