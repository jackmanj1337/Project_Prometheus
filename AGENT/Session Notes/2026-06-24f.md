  independent knobs**). All `CampaignRules`-tunable under a **per-map cap** (anti-grind).

  pairwise affinity tables); reuses `active_modifiers`/Charm aura vocab; **proximity-gated aura**
  (one radius primitive now powers proximity gain + class-EXP share + the benefit aura); **scales by
  rank**; **stacks independently** of pair-up bonus + Charm.
- **Top rank:** **exclusive** (one partner — FE S-rank/marriage slot), behind a `CampaignRules`
  **toggle**; **Avatar (#20) hooks this slot**.
