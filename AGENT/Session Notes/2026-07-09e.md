## Scope Boundary (why it stopped here)

The vocabulary unification touches no save schema, so it's unblocked. The
schema-affecting parts — `UnitData.extra_stats` dict storage, `ClassData`
extra-stat bases/caps/growths, the `get_effective_stat` fallback, and the
author-declared `CampaignRules` stat registry ([STM-3], STM §3 F1 reserves) — must
NOT front-run the F1 lock. Adding an author stat is therefore **not yet one
entry**; this slice just removes the "edit 7 lists in lockstep" trap.

Two more stat-ish sites were inspected and **correctly left out**:
`PairUpBonusTable.scaling_stats` is an author `@export` (a deliberately different,
configurable subset), and `UnitDetailsScreen`'s stat pairs are UI layout, not the
vocabulary.
