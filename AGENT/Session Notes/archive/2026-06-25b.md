## `[CEX-24]` Universal `no_attack` source — RESOLVED
The **inverse of `fists`** (`[CEX-20]`): infinite, never-breaking, **empty effect set** → **cannot
initiate AND cannot counter**. Headline use: **don't kill a weak recruitable on your own counterattack**
(an alternative to the `[STY-6]` non-lethal capture style — just don't strike back).
- **Mandatory & universal:** in every unit's known sources — a built-in granted row on all units
  (recomputed like `class_innate`, **not saved**, **not removable**). Player label author-skinnable
  (default **"Restrain"**); internal id `no_attack`.
- **When equipped:** **Attack** unavailable, `can_counterattack()` → false; other actions (Staff/Use,
  Item) unaffected (it blocks *attacks*, not the turn). Takes **no styles**.
- **In the `[CEX-22]` fallback queue:** a valid equip target + queue entry, but **defaults to the
  BOTTOM** (below `fists`) so a breaking weapon never makes a unit accidentally pacifist; the player
  promotes/equips it to opt in. Picking it as the floor makes "**counter with nothing**" an *explicit,
  equippable* state, not just the absence of a usable source.
- **Save (F1):** none new (universal built-in; `equipped_source` + `[CEX-22]` MRU/ordering already cover
  referencing it).
