## `[STY-17]` Faction relationship matrix ("aggression matrix") — RESOLVED
- Upgrade `are_hostile` (binary/symmetric) → an author-defined **DIRECTED 3-state matrix**:
  `relationship(from,to) ∈ {hostile|neutral|allied}` (A→B may differ from B→A). Enables **neutral
  NPCs** + **one-way / passive-until-provoked** aggression. Mirrors the `[CEX-11]` triangle matrix.
- **Default** derives from today's alliance groups (non-breaking; no neutral by default).
- **Authoring:** per-`FactionData` stance overrides on `MapData`, fallback to group derivation.
- **`target_filter` resolution:** `enemy`=hostile, `ally`=allied, `neutral`=neutral, `any`=ignore (FF),
  `self`. **Cross-faction is free** — `enemy` spans all hostile factions (blue AoE hits red+yellow,
  not green).
- **Shared:** a **faction-model (M14) extension** consumed by targeting AND `EnemyAI`/EXP/objectives;
  `are_hostile()` becomes a shim over `relationship()==hostile`.
- **Counter vs stance (lean):** being attacked counters regardless of stance (self-defense); neutral/
  allied AI just won't *initiate*.
- **Save (F1):** authored matrix = map data; **reserve a runtime relationship-override store** for
  provoked/`[MET]` changes.
