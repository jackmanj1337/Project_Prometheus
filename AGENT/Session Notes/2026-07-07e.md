### V028-03 / Q2 — root-cause menu re-center (the recurring bug)

- `MenuScale._recenter` now installs a **reactive `resized` re-center**: each centered
  panel's own `resized` signal drives `_on_centered_target_resized` → `_recenter`, so
  centering re-runs at the exact frame the engine settles the panel size. A
  `_RECENTER_GUARD_META` re-entrancy flag skips the nested pass emitted by our own
  `target.size` write; a `_RESIZE_HOOKED_META` flag connects the signal once per target.
- This replaces the one-shot imperative offset-bake that went stale on the deferred
  layout pass and had been patched per-trigger four times (V025-05a / V026-01a /
  V027-04a / V028-03 maximize).
- **Deviation from the literal decision (flagged):** I did NOT fully retire the viewport
  `size_changed` hook. On investigation it does two jobs besides centering — the V021-08
  fit-clamp (re-scale a menu down when the window *shrinks*) and the OS-resize
  write-back. Removing it would regress the window-shrink fit case, so I kept it and
  re-scoped its comment to "fit-clamp + write-back only". The reactive hook is the
  standing centering constraint; the viewport hook centering is now redundant/harmless.
  The clean structural form (CenterContainer, delete imperative `_recenter`) stays
  deferred to `UI-VIEWPORT-ASPECT`.

### V028-03 / Q2 — maximize write-back policy (Option A)

- `SettingsManager.resize_write_back_action(ds_mode, last_mode)` — a pure,
  headless-testable policy: `ignore` while maximized (never persist the maximized client
  size), `restore` on the maximize→windowed transition (re-apply the saved windowed
  size), `write_back` only for a genuine windowed edge drag. `_maybe_write_back_os_resize`
  now supplies the live `DisplayServer` reads and tracks `_last_window_mode`.

### V028-02 / Q1 — structured window-size status

- `SettingsManager.windowed_size_status()` returns `{kind: preset|custom, requested,
  applied}` so the readout stops conflating a preset **request** (clamp may shrink it)
  with a custom **observed client size** (already applied). `SettingsScreen._refresh_applied_size`
  renders `client WxH` for custom values and never re-runs them through the 16:9 clamp —
  killing the `Custom (3840x2071) → applied 3563x2004` nonsense.
