## What we firmed — `[CEX-13]` (pure recharge)
- **`ConsumableComponent.uses_per_map: int`** (-1 = not a per-map item).
- When set, the item is **pure-recharge**: `InventoryEntry.uses_remaining` stays **-1** (never
  consumed); a **per-instance `InventoryEntry.map_uses_remaining`** counter — refilled to
  `uses_per_map` by `reset_map_state` at map start (mirrors `skill_use_counters`) — gates each use.
  **Per-instance** (two trinkets track independently). **No finite total cap in v1.**
- **Owner decisions:** (1) **pure recharge** (permanent), not recharge+finite-cap; (2) **distinct
  "N/max ⟳" badge** + "resets each map" tooltip in inventory + action menu; consumed items show `×N`.
- **Future (out of v1):** per-N-turns / charge-on-rest intervals.
