## 3. The [DCH-2] override and its consequence
Owner chose **unify** over the recommended split (doors-as-terrain-code + chests-as-object-list).
What this changes for the build:
- **Doors are NOT terrain codes.** Passability must come from a **runtime overlay derived from
  `map_objects` state** — `GridManager`/pathfinding treats a closed-or-locked door tile as
  blocked, then re-opens it when the door's `open`/`locked` flag flips. **This is slice 1's main
  work** (bespoke door-passability code, instead of reusing `get_move_cost`/`is_passable` free).
- **Upside bought:** the authored terrain `grid` stays immutable; `map_objects_state` becomes the
  SINGLE home for both door open-state and chest looted-state, which **fully resolves the
  `MapData` runtime-terrain save-TODO** (no terrain mutation at all).
- **STW inherits this:** [STW-1]'s rec A (reuse `map_objects` with a `type:"siege_weapon"` entry)
  is now the confirmed path; DCH slice 1 builds the tile-occupancy/passability overlay STW rides.
