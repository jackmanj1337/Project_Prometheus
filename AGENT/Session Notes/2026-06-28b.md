## Research takeaways
- Recommendation: do not build a general sandboxed scripting language for v1.
- Build the cheap seam first: `ActivityRegistry`, `launch_activity`, `ActivityRunner`, typed
  context/result schemas, reward bounds, and the result-to-effect bridge.
- Keep first-party activities as trusted Godot scenes initially.
- If public author scripting is needed later, prototype declarative templates first, then evaluate a
  small MiniScript/Wren-like VM only if templates are insufficient.
- Avoid untrusted GDScript, `.tscn`, or Godot `Expression` for public packs; they cross the existing
  raw-load-art-only content-pack boundary.
