## What was done

Built **`B1-SAVECODEC` Slice 4** on `v0.3.0-features`.

`scripts/save/SaveCodec.gd` now owns the pure JSON-safe serializer surface for
Retry-facing unit and inventory state:

- `Vector2i` serializes as `{x, y}` dictionaries.
- `InventoryEntry` serializes as dictionaries, including type/id, uses,
  `forged_mods`, and equip modifier fields.
- `UnitData` Retry snapshots serialize through `SaveCodec.unit_data_to_dict`
  and restore through `SaveCodec.apply_unit_dict`.
- Snapshot validation moved behind `SaveCodec.validate_unit_snapshot_dict`.

  `_validate_snapshot_unit_dict` now delegate to the codec while the public
  `take_map_snapshot` / `restore_map_snapshot` behavior stays stable.
- Added `DataManager.has_weapon()` so snapshot/reference validation can check
  weapon ids without calling `get_weapon()` and producing unrelated push errors.
